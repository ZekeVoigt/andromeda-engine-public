Add a slugify() helper to ansible's text utilities.

Create `lib/ansible/module_utils/common/text/slugify.py` with a function
`slugify(text, separator='-')` that turns any text into a URL- and filename-safe slug:

- lowercase the text;
- fold accented letters to their plain ASCII form ("Café" -> "cafe");
- replace every run of characters that are not ASCII letters or digits with `separator`;
- drop leading and trailing separators.

For example, `slugify('Hello, World!')` is `'hello-world'` and
`slugify('a b c', separator='_')` is `'a_b_c'`. Do not change any existing module.
